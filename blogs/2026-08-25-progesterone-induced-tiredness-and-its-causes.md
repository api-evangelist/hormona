Progesterone and its sedative properties Your brain relies on specific chemical signals to regulate your sleep and wake cycles. Progesterone acts on these systems as a natural sedative. This relaxing hormone prepares your body for rest, so feeling drowsy when your levels shift is a normal biological response rather than a sign of weakness.

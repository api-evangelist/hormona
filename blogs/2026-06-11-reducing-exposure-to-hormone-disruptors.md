Mimicking natural hormone signaling Endocrine disrupting chemicals, or EDCs, are synthetic substances that interfere with your natural hormone signaling. Your endocrine system works like a lock and key, where hormones fit precisely into cellular receptors. EDCs trick your body by mimicking natural hormones, blocking these receptors, or directly altering hormone levels [1].

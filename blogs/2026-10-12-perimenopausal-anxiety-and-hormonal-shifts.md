Hormonal shifts and anxiety triggers If you are wondering, can perimenopause cause anxiety, the answer is deeply rooted in biology. This mid-life transition brings erratic shifts to your endocrine system, making it a highly common trigger for sudden mood changes. It’s completely understandable to feel terrified when your mental state shifts without warning.

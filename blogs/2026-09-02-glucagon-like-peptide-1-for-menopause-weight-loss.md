Menopause transition and metabolic shifts The years leading up to your final period trigger sharp hormonal fluctuations. This phase, known as perimenopause, alters your metabolism and changes how your body stores energy. As your ovaries produce less estrogen, your body looks for new ways to maintain hormonal balance.

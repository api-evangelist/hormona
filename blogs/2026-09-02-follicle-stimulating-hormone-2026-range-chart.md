FSH production and function Your reproductive system operates on a constant feedback loop between your brain and your ovaries. The process starts in your brain, where the pituitary gland produces follicle-stimulating hormone (FSH). As the name suggests, this hormone travels to your ovaries and signals them to grow and mature an egg for ovulation.

Common chemicals in personal care Endocrine disruptors are chemicals that mimic or interfere with natural hormones [1]. Your skin absorbs many of these compounds directly into your bloodstream. This makes topical application a high-risk exposure pathway because it bypasses the digestive filters of your liver [2].
